How it works

1

Apply

Tell us about your kitchen and upload your documents. Takes about five minutes on your phone.

2

Tasting and visit

We taste your food, check hygiene and your documents, then score you on a simple scorecard.

3

Go live

You are listed to customers near you. You choose which approved dishes you cook each day.

4

Cook and get paid

Orders arrive on your phone before each meal. We deliver. You are paid weekly for what was delivered.

What you get

  • Customers without advertising. We market your kitchen to your own estate.
  • Orders ahead of time, so you cook what is already sold and waste less.
  • Delivery arranged by Azizo.
  • Fresh beef, goat and chicken from Azizo if you want it.
  • Training, packaging guidance and a clear recipe card for every dish.
  • A transparent weekly statement showing every order and our commission.

What we ask

  • A clean kitchen and a valid food handler's medical certificate.
  • A business permit and public health licence for your premises (we help you through it).
  • Cook the approved dishes the Azizo way: same portions, same packaging.
  • Confirm orders quickly and have food ready at handover time.
  • Be honest. Hygiene problems or repeated no-shows lead to delisting.

You stay independent: your own business, your own team, your own hours when you are not on an order. Azizo charges a commission only on food sold through the platform (currently 20%, shown to you before you start).

Common questions

Do I pay to join?

No joining fee. Azizo keeps a commission only on food sold through the platform.

Will I be an Azizo employee?

No. You are an independent business. You decide whether to accept each order and you manage your own kitchen and helpers.

Who delivers?

Azizo arranges delivery. You pack the food to our standard and hand it over.

When am I paid?

Weekly, for delivered orders, after commission. Your portal shows every order and your balance.

What if my area is full?

You join a waiting list in the order you applied and we contact you when a place opens.
