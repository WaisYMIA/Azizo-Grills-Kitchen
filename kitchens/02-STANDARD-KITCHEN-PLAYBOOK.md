# Azizo Standard Kitchen Playbook, v1

Principle: *a customer in Utawala and a customer in Umoja get the same plate.* Prices are fixed by Azizo and are starting points to test in the pilot (editable in Kitchens Admin). The portion weights below are cooked weights.

## A. Daily routine (every kitchen)
1. Wash hands (20 seconds, soap), clean apron or tabard, hair covered, no jewellery on hands. Anyone with diarrhoea, vomiting, fever or an open wound does not handle food that day.
2. Check the day's orders in the Kitchen Portal before 08:30. Mark any dish sold out.
3. Cook only what is ordered plus a small buffer. Cook lunch between 10:00 and 12:00; dinner between 16:00 and 18:00.
4. Weigh portions. Pack. Label. Take a photo of the finished packed order (Portal checklist).
5. Mark **Ready** the moment the food is packed. Hand over to the rider within 10 minutes.
6. Clean down, empty bins, record the fridge temperature (below 4 C).

## B. Food safety rules
- **Cook to temperature:** chicken 74 C; minced meat 71 C; pork 63 C with a 3-minute rest; all other meats and fish until cooked through. Use a probe thermometer.
- **Hold time:** hot food is packed above 60 C. Eat-within time is **2 hours** from cooking (fish 1 hour). Anything older is thrown away, never sold.
- **Cold chain:** kachumbari and raw ingredients below 4 C.
- **Separation:** raw meat on the bottom shelf; separate boards and knives for raw and cooked; wash hands between.
- **Water and cleaning:** drinking-quality water only; sanitise surfaces before each shift.
- **Allergens:** tell customers about peanuts, milk (biryani raita), and fish. Note any special request on the ticket.
- **Pests and waste:** covered bins, no pets in the kitchen, pest control as required by the county.
- **Records:** keep medical certificates, licences and the fridge log; Azizo may inspect without notice.

## C. Packaging standard
Sturdy, leak-proof food-grade containers (as listed per dish), gravy in a separate cup, hot items vented slightly, sealed bag with the order ticket stapled outside. Azizo-branded bags and stickers are supplied at cost.

## D. Delivery handover standard
Rider collects within 10 minutes of Ready. Kitchen hands over the bag sealed. Delivery target: within the slot window. If the kitchen cannot meet the slot, it must reject the order before the cutoff, not after.

## E. Scorecard and strikes
Each kitchen is scored out of 100 each month: customer rating (40), on-time ready rate (20), order acceptance rate (15), complaints and refunds (15), inspection results (10). Under 70 means a warning; under 60 for two months, or one serious food-safety incident, means suspension pending review. Strikes and decisions are recorded in Kitchens Admin.

## F. Dish cards

### Beef Stew & Ugali Combo
*Pan-Kenyan · Combo · Lunch and dinner · KSh 450 · serves 1*

Tender beef in a thick tomato and onion gravy with ugali, sukuma wiki and kachumbari.

**Portion standard (weigh it)**
- Beef stew: 180 g cooked beef + 200 ml gravy
- Ugali: 300 g (or rice 250 g)
- Sukuma wiki: 120 g
- Kachumbari: 60 g

**Method standard**
1. Beef simmered until fork-tender (min 90 min), never fried dry
2. Gravy: onion, tomato, garlic, ginger, salt only; no stock cubes beyond 1/4 cube per kg
3. Sukuma sliced thin, cooked 6-8 minutes so it stays green
4. Kachumbari made fresh the same morning

**Packaging:** 700 ml container + 250 ml gravy cup. **Label:** dish name, kitchen name, cooked time, "eat within 2 hours".

**Note:** Beef bought from Azizo.
