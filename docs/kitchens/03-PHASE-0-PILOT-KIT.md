# Phase 0: paper pilot kit (2 to 3 weeks)

Goal: prove that customers will pay Azizo prices for homemade Kenyan lunch, and that cooks can hit the standard, **before** we scale. The software (Phase 1) supports it, but the pilot can run on WhatsApp and the existing website.

## Setup
- **Cooks:** 3 cooks, one near each of three live branches (suggest Umoja, Buruburu, Utawala). Start with 6 dishes each: Beef Stew & Ugali, Chicken Stew & Rice, Pilau Beef, Githeri Special, Chapati & Ndengu, Mukimo & Beef Stew.
- **Meat:** Azizo supplies the beef and chicken at wholesale; record every kilogram.
- **Orders:** customers order by WhatsApp (script below) or on the Kitchens page; one coordinator confirms each order with the cook.
- **Delivery:** use the branch's existing rider. Record the cost per delivery.

## Selection scorecard for cooks (score 1-5)
| Area | What to check |
|---|---|
| Taste | tasting of 3 dishes, scored by 2 tasters |
| Consistency | cook the same dish twice on different days; compare weight and look |
| Hygiene | handwashing, surfaces, storage, water, waste |
| Premises | space, ventilation, fridge, separate prep areas |
| Documents | ID, KRA PIN, medical certificate, permit or a plan to get one in 30 days |
| Reliability | references, phone responsiveness, availability for lunch peak |
| Attitude | accepts standards, honest about limits |
Accept only cooks averaging 4 or more with no 1 in hygiene or documents.

## Kitchen inspection checklist (tick on site)
Clean water supply; handwash station with soap; no pets in kitchen; fridge below 4 C; raw and cooked separate; covered bins; pest signs none; probe thermometer present; cooking area clean; packaging stored off the floor; fire extinguisher or plan; medical certificates visible.

## Daily pilot routine
- 09:00 coordinator confirms the day's lunch count with each cook.
- 10:30 order cutoff.
- 12:00-14:00 delivery window.
- 14:30 collect feedback and ratings; log issues.
- Weekly review: sales, rejects, lateness, complaints, rating, delivery cost, meat used.

## WhatsApp order script
Customer: "Lunch order: 1 Beef Stew & Ugali, 1 Githeri Special. Delivery to [estate/building], phone [number], time 1pm."
Reply: "Thank you! Total KSh [x] including delivery KSh [y]. Pay on delivery (cash or M-Pesa). Your cook is [name]. Order number [n]."

## KPIs and go / no-go (after 3 weeks)
| Measure | Go if |
|---|---|
| Orders per cook per day | at least 8 by week 3 |
| Customer rating | 4.3 or higher |
| Orders ready on time | 90% or more |
| Reject or cancel rate | under 8% |
| Complaints | under 3% of orders, none serious |
| Delivery cost | under 25% of the order value (or fee covers it) |
| Margin | 20% commission plus meat margin covers Azizo costs |
| Repeat customers | at least 25% order again in week 3 |
If three or more fail, fix the cause before onboarding more cooks.
