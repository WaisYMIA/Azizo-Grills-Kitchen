# Kitchen Partner Agreement: term sheet (draft for your advocate)

This is a negotiation outline, not a contract. It reflects the independent-marketplace structure in the memo.

1. **Parties and relationship.** Azizo (platform and brand) and the Kitchen Partner (an independent food business). No employment, partnership, agency or franchise relationship. The partner uses their own judgement on how to run their business, hours and staff, within the quality rules.
2. **Services.** Azizo provides the online ordering platform, customer marketing, order routing, payment collection and settlement. The partner prepares and packs food to the Azizo standard.
3. **Standards.** The Playbook (portions, ingredients, packaging, hold times) applies and may be updated with 14 days' notice. Azizo may sample, taste and inspect with reasonable notice (immediately after a complaint).
4. **Licences.** Partner keeps valid business registration, KRA PIN, county permits, public health licence and food handler certificates for everyone who handles food, and gives copies to Azizo. Failure to renew means automatic suspension.
5. **Menu and prices.** Partner sells only approved dishes. Prices are set by Azizo for brand consistency (lawyer: confirm that a platform-set consumer price is lawful under competition law; alternatives are recommended prices or a price band).
6. **Meat supply.** The partner buys meat for platform dishes from Azizo butcheries at agreed prices (lawyer: confirm this does not amount to an unlawful tie; make it a quality requirement with a fall-back to approved suppliers).
7. **Orders.** Partner accepts or rejects within the set time. Reject rate and lateness are scored. Capacity is set by the partner.
8. **Fees and payment.** Azizo pays the partner the order food value minus the commission (default 20%), weekly by M-Pesa from the Kitchens statement. Refunds caused by the partner's fault are deducted. Azizo issues commission invoices.
9. **Delivery.** Pilot: Azizo arranges riders. The partner hands over sealed, labelled food on time. Responsibility moves to the rider at handover.
10. **Liability and insurance.** The partner is responsible for the safety of the food they prepare and indemnifies Azizo against claims caused by it. Both parties hold appropriate product liability insurance (to be quoted).
11. **Complaints.** A customer complaint triggers a refund or replacement decided under the published policy; repeated or serious cases lead to suspension.
12. **Non-exclusivity.** The partner may sell through other channels, including other delivery platforms.
13. **Brand and recipes.** Azizo name and logo are licensed for use on packaging and listings only. Azizo's Playbook belongs to Azizo; the partner's own family recipes remain theirs.
14. **Data.** Customer details are shared only to fulfil the order and must be deleted after the dispute period. Both parties comply with the Data Protection Act 2019.
15. **Term and termination.** Open-ended with 30 days' notice by either side. Immediate suspension for food-safety risk, expired licences, fraud or abuse of customers. Delisting follows the scorecard rules.
16. **Disputes.** Escalation, then mediation, then the courts of Kenya (or arbitration if your advocate prefers).
Items marked "lawyer" need a legal opinion before signing.
