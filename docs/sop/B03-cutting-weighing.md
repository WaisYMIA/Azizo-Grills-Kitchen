# Cutting, portioning and weighing
Scope: Butchery

Document AZ-SOP-B03 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Purpose
Fair weights, clean cuts, no cross-contamination.
## Steps
1. Colour-coded boards and knives: red = raw red meat, yellow = poultry, blue = fish, white = cooked or ready to eat.
2. Wash and sanitise boards, knives and the saw between species, and every time you pause longer than 30 minutes.
3. Keep only what you can cut in 20 minutes out of the chiller. Return the rest at once.
4. Scales are checked each morning with a test weight and the county weights and measures stamp must be valid. Tare the tray first. Weigh in front of the customer, and show the screen.
5. Quote price per kg and the weighed total. Where the customer ordered by estimate online, the final price follows the true weight and the customer is told before delivery.
6. Pack in clean, food-grade bags or trays; label with product, weight, date and branch.
## Records
Scale check (daily), cleaning sign-off.
## If something goes wrong
Scale error: stop using it, switch to the backup scale, tell the manager.
