# Complaints, refunds and product recall
Scope: Butchery, Ready to Eat, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-B08 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Promise to customers
"Not happy? We replace or refund within 24 hours."
## Steps
1. Listen, apologise, record the order number, photo and what was wrong. Do not argue.
2. Within 24 hours: replace the item, or refund through M-Pesa. Quality and safety complaints are always accepted.
3. If the complaint is about illness: stop selling that batch, keep samples, write down the batch and supplier, and tell Azizo head office immediately through a support ticket marked urgent.
4. Recall: Azizo head office decides. The partner pulls the batch, lists the customers from order records, and contacts them the same day.
5. Log the cause and the fix. Three complaints of the same kind in 30 days trigger an unannounced inspection.
## Records
Complaint register, refund log, recall record.
