# Support and incident escalation
Scope: All

Document AZ-SOP-G03 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Channels
Portal support ticket for everything non-urgent (we reply within 1 working day). Mark urgent only for safety, payment or customer-illness issues (we reply within 2 hours in opening hours). WhatsApp for the live shop line.
## Levels
1. Branch manager: fixes within the shift.
2. Azizo head office: anything involving food safety, money, legal or the brand.
3. Authorities: serious illness, a death, a fire, or a legal notice — notify Azizo head office first and cooperate with the county public health officer.
## Incident record
Date, time, what happened, who was affected, what was done, what will change. Kept for 12 months.
