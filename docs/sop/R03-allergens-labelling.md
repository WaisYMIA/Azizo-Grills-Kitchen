# Allergen management and labelling
Scope: Ready to Eat, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-R03 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Purpose
Prevent allergic reactions and be honest about what is in the food.
## Steps
1. Know the main allergens in your menu: milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, peanuts and tree nuts, soy, wheat or gluten, sesame, and sulphites. For each dish, keep a list of the allergens in its ingredients and sauces. Review whenever a recipe or supplier changes.
2. Tell customers: on the menu, "Please tell us about any allergy before you order". Staff must be able to say what is in any dish. Never guess; say "I will check".
3. Prevent contact: use separate utensils, clean hands and surfaces, and fresh oil when preparing a dish for an allergic customer. Mark the order.
4. Pack and label takeaway dishes with the dish name, date, and allergens present.
5. In an emergency (swelling, difficulty breathing): call an ambulance (112 or 999) at once, and stay with the person.
## Records
Allergen matrix per dish, staff training record.
