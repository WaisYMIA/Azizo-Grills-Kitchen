# Azizo Kitchens: food handling and temperature log
Scope: Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-K02 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Receive ingredients from named suppliers; check dates and condition; chill or freeze within 15 minutes.
2. Cook to the temperatures in R01. Hold and cool as in R02.
3. Keep raw and cooked separate, with colour-coded boards, in the fridge and on the counter.
4. Write temperatures for the fridge (morning, midday, evening), and for each batch of cooked food.
5. Label every container with name, date and use-by. Discard past use-by.
## Records
Fridge log, batch log, receiving log; keep them for 12 months.
