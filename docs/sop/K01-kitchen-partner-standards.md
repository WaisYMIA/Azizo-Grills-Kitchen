# Azizo Kitchens: partner standards
Scope: Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-K01 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Who can be an Azizo Kitchen
Licensed home or commercial kitchens that pass the Azizo hygiene inspection (score 70 or more with no critical fail) and complete onboarding.
## Standards
1. Premises: a separate food area with washable surfaces, screened windows, a hand-wash basin, potable water and a working fridge. No pets or children in the cooking area.
2. People: valid food-handler medical certificates for everyone who cooks; the hygiene rules in B04.
3. Menu: only dishes approved by Azizo. Photos and descriptions must match the food. Allergens as in R03.
4. Prices and availability are kept up to date in the portal. Mark a dish unavailable the moment you run out.
5. Insurance and licence: the business licence and any county health certificate must be current.
6. Brand: use the Azizo name only as the brand guidelines allow (G01).
## Consequences
Missing standards lead to a written warning, an unannounced inspection, then suspension.
