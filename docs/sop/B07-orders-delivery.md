# Customer orders and delivery
Scope: Butchery

Document AZ-SOP-B07 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Acknowledge each online order in the app within 5 minutes. Update the status honestly: Received → Preparing → Ready → Out for delivery → Completed.
2. Pick and pack to the order. Weigh each item, label it, pack raw meat separately from other items, add ice packs for journeys beyond 30 minutes.
3. Check before handover: items, weights, notes, price, address. A second person checks orders above KES 5,000.
4. Riders: clean insulated bag, clean hands, helmet and reflective jacket, no meat on the open bike, deliver within the quoted slot. Call the customer when you are 5 minutes away.
5. Take cash or confirm the M-Pesa message on the customer's phone. Give a receipt.
6. Out of stock: tell the customer before you substitute. Mark the item sold out in the portal immediately.
## Records
Order history in the portal, rider log.
## Service targets
Prepare within 25 minutes, deliver within the slot, 95% of orders complete without a complaint.
