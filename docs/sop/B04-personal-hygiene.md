# Personal hygiene and dress
Scope: Butchery, Ready to Eat, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-B04 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Wash hands (soap, warm water, 20 seconds, dry with a single-use towel) on arrival, after the toilet, after touching raw meat, waste, phones or money, and before handling ready-to-eat food.
2. Clean apron, hair covering and closed non-slip shoes. Beard covering where needed. No jewellery other than a plain band, short clean nails, no nail polish.
3. Cuts must be covered by a blue waterproof plaster and a glove.
4. Anyone with diarrhoea, vomiting, a fever, a sore throat with fever, or infected skin must not handle food. Tell the manager the same day. Return only after 48 hours without symptoms.
5. No eating, smoking, chewing or spitting in the work area.
6. Staff hold a valid medical certificate (food handler) renewed every 6 months, as required by the county.
## Records
Staff medical certificate register, sickness log.
## If something goes wrong
A sick worker is sent home on full pay for that day: the shop's reputation is worth more.
