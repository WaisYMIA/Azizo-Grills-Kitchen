# Pest control, waste and water
Scope: Butchery, Ready to Eat, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-B06 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Doors, windows, vents and drains are screened or sealed. Doors are kept shut. No standing water. Bait stations and fly killers are placed away from open food and checked weekly.
2. Hire a licensed pest controller at least quarterly; keep the certificate and the visit report.
3. Waste: closed, lined, foot-pedal bins in the work area, emptied at least daily (and when three-quarters full). Offal, bones and fat are held in a covered container until collected by a licensed handler. Never burn or dump.
4. Water: only potable water in the cutting and wash areas. If the supply is from a tank or borehole, test it every 6 months and clean the tank every 3 months. Keep the result.
5. Hand-wash basins have soap, towels and hot water, and are used only for hands.
## Records
Pest control certificate, water test, waste collection receipts.
## If something goes wrong
Signs of pests: stop, clean, call the controller the same day and record it.
