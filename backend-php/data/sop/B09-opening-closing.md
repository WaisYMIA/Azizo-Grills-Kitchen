# Opening and closing checklist
Scope: Butchery, Ready to Eat

Document AZ-SOP-B09 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Opening
Staff hygiene check; read and log chiller and freezer temperatures; check the scale; clean and sanitise surfaces; check stock against the portal and mark sold-out items; check the till float and M-Pesa phone; read overnight orders.
## Closing
Final temperature reading; clean and sanitise everything; wrap and date leftovers or freeze them the same day; empty bins; lock stores; record the day's takings and orders; update tomorrow's stock in the portal; sign the sheet.
## Records
Opening and closing sheet, signed daily.
