# Brand standards
Scope: All

Document AZ-SOP-G01 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Name
Always "Azizo Butchery and Grill" for the main brand and "Azizo Kitchens" for the kitchens network. Partners say "Partner of Azizo Butchery and Grill". Never use the name for any other business.
## Look
Colours: gold and black on dark backgrounds with red accents. Use the Azizo logo supplied in the portal, unaltered, with clear space equal to the height of the letter A. Do not stretch, recolour or add effects.
## Voice
Warm, direct and respectful. Short sentences. Prices and weights are always clear.
## Shopfront and uniform
Azizo sign with the inspection grade card shown inside (if issued). Clean aprons in the brand colours, name badge, closed shoes. Delivery bags are branded and clean.
## Photos
Real photos of your own food and shop. No photos taken from other sellers. Always show fresh product on clean surfaces.
## Social media
Only post information about your branch with Azizo's agreement. Never promise a discount that has not been approved.
