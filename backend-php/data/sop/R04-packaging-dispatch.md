# Packaging and dispatch of ready-to-eat food
Scope: Ready to Eat, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-R04 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Use clean, food-grade, leak-proof containers. Do not reuse single-use packaging. Keep hot and cold items apart.
2. Seal with a tamper-evident sticker, and add the dish name, the time packed, and "Eat within 2 hours".
3. Hand over to a rider in a clean insulated bag. Cooked food is handed over at 60°C or above; cold items at 5°C or below.
4. Check the order against the ticket before it leaves: items, sauces, cutlery, notes, allergy flag.
5. Cap delivery at 45 minutes from packing. Beyond that, remake the order.
