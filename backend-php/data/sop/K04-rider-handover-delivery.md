# Azizo Kitchens: rider handover and delivery hygiene
Scope: Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-K04 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Riders wear clean clothes, wash hands before each pick-up, and use a clean insulated bag and carry no other goods in it.
2. Check the seal and the order ticket at pick-up. Refuse unsealed food.
3. Go straight to the customer. No personal stops. Share the live location if the customer asks.
4. Hand over, say the order number, take payment if it is cash on delivery and return the money to the kitchen or Azizo as agreed.
5. If the customer is unreachable: call twice over 10 minutes, then message and call the kitchen. Do not leave food unattended or sell it elsewhere.
6. Report accidents or spills at once; replace the order.
