# Azizo Kitchens: order acceptance and preparation time
Scope: Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-K03 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Accept or reject a new order within 5 minutes. A rejection needs a reason (out of stock, too busy, outside hours).
2. Confirm the preparation time honestly and keep to it. Update the status: Accepted → Preparing → Ready.
3. Mark the order Ready only when it is packed and sealed (R04). The rider must not wait more than 10 minutes.
4. A customer cancellation before preparation starts is free. After preparation starts, the kitchen is paid for the food prepared.
5. Missed or late orders are reviewed weekly. Three late orders in a week trigger a coaching call.
## Service targets
Acceptance in 5 minutes, ready on time 90% of orders, rating 4.3 or higher.
