# Franchise onboarding and go-live
Scope: Butchery, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-G02 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Journey
1. Apply: owner details, premises, licence, health certificate, payment method.
2. Review: Azizo checks the business licence, the health certificate and the owner's ID. Documents are private and never published.
3. Pre-approval visit: an Azizo inspector completes the quality and hygiene inspection form (form AZ/QHI/001).
4. Onboarding: read the SOP handbook, complete the training, sign the partner agreement, confirm the premises photos and payment details.
5. Items: list at least 5 items with prices.
6. Go-live gate: Azizo head office checks the checklist. All items must be ticked and the inspection must score 70 or more with no critical fail. An override needs a written reason, which is recorded.
7. Trial period (pilot), then live.
## After go-live
Routine inspection on the interval set by the grade (90+ every 180 days, 80 every 120, 70 every 90). A 60–69 score means a 30-day fix plan; below 60 means suspension and a re-inspection within 7 days.
