# Cold chain and storage
Scope: Butchery

Document AZ-SOP-B02 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Purpose
Keep meat cold enough, long enough, from delivery to customer.
## Steps
1. Chillers at 0–4°C, freezers at -18°C or colder. Check and write down the temperature at opening, midday and closing. Use a thermometer you can read without opening the door.
2. Raw and ready-to-eat food are stored separately: raw below, cooked above. Red meat, poultry and fish are kept apart. Nothing touches the floor.
3. First in, first out. Fresh beef, goat and pork: sell or freeze within 3 days of receipt. Poultry and mince: 2 days. Fish: 1 day. Ice-pack cool-boxes for deliveries longer than 30 minutes.
4. Never refreeze thawed meat. Thaw only in the chiller, never on the counter.
5. Keep doors shut; do not overload. Defrost freezers on schedule.
6. Power cut: keep the doors closed, tell the manager, move stock to the backup unit or ice boxes, and discard chilled meat that has been above 5°C for more than 2 hours.
## Records
Temperature log (3 times daily), discard log.
## Critical limits
Chiller above 5°C or freezer above -15°C for more than 2 hours = act and log it.
## If something goes wrong
Move the stock, call the technician, log the time and the stock affected, and throw away anything doubtful.
