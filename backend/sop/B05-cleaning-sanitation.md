# Cleaning and sanitation schedule
Scope: Butchery, Ready to Eat, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-B05 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Clean, then sanitise. Remove debris, wash with detergent, rinse, apply a food-safe sanitiser at the label strength, leave for the contact time, rinse if the label says so.
2. After every shift: tables, blocks, knives, saws, scales, mincer, floors, drains. Daily: chillers wiped, bins washed. Weekly: chiller and freezer shelves emptied and cleaned, walls and ceiling. Monthly: deep clean, drains descaled.
3. Cleaning cloths: single-use or colour-coded and washed hot daily. Mops are kept separate from the food area.
4. Keep chemicals in a labelled, closed cupboard away from food. Never decant into drink bottles.
5. The supervisor signs the cleaning sheet each shift.
## Records
Cleaning schedule signed daily, chemical stock list.
