# Order failure handling (payment, no-answer, wrong item)
Scope: All

Document AZ-SOP-G04 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Payment failed or doubtful
Check the M-Pesa message and the portal status. If money left the customer's phone but the order shows unpaid, record the M-Pesa code and the time, then raise an urgent ticket. Never ask the customer to pay twice.
## Customer not answering
Call twice 5 minutes apart, then send a WhatsApp message. Wait 15 minutes. Keep chilled items cold. If still no contact, return the order; record it as "no answer". Perishable raw meat must not be re-sold if it has been out of the cold chain for more than 2 hours.
## Wrong or missing item
Apologise, deliver the correct item free within the same day or refund it. Record the cause.
## Late order
Tell the customer before the slot ends, with a new time. Late by more than 45 minutes: offer a discount or delivery refund.
## Repeated problems
Escalate to head office; they review the pattern weekly.
