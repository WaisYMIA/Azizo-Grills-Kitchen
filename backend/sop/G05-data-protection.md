# Data protection (Kenya Data Protection Act 2019)
Scope: All

Document AZ-SOP-G05 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Principles
Collect only what is needed (name, phone, address, order). Use it only to serve the order and, if the customer agrees, to send offers. Keep it secure. Delete it when asked.
## Rules
1. Never share customer details outside Azizo and its delivery riders for that order.
2. Do not copy customer lists to personal phones or social media.
3. Passwords are personal. Never share them. Sign out on shared devices.
4. Documents (licences, ID) are private and are not uploaded or posted.
5. Requests: customers can ask for a copy of their data, deletion, or to stop marketing messages. The portal logs these requests. We reply within 7 days.
6. Breach: if you suspect a data leak or lost phone with the portal signed in, tell Azizo head office at once.
## Note
Azizo should confirm whether it must register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
