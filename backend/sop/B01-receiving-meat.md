# Receiving livestock and meat
Scope: Butchery

Document AZ-SOP-B01 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Purpose
Make sure only safe, legal, traceable meat enters the shop.
## Who
Butchery manager (accepts or rejects) and the receiving attendant.
## Steps
1. Buy only from licensed abattoirs or suppliers. Keep their licence copy on file and check the expiry every month.
2. Every delivery must have a stamped meat inspection certificate or delivery note showing supplier, date, species and weight. No paper, no acceptance.
3. Check the vehicle: clean, covered, meat off the floor, chilled or iced where the trip is longer than 2 hours.
4. Check the meat: bright colour for the species, no sour or off smell, no slime, no bruising or abscesses, firm texture. Probe core temperature of chilled meat: accept at 4°C or below (reject above 7°C). Frozen meat: accept at -15°C or below with no thaw signs.
5. Weigh and record: date, supplier, species, kg, temperature, accepted or rejected, receiver's name.
6. Move to the chiller or freezer within 15 minutes. Label each batch with the received date (first in, first out).
## Records
Receiving log (daily), supplier licence file, rejection notes.
## Critical limits
Chilled 4°C or below. Frozen -18°C target. Any doubt about origin = reject.
## If something goes wrong
Reject and photograph the load, call the supplier, record it in the log, and tell the owner. Never "sell it quickly".
