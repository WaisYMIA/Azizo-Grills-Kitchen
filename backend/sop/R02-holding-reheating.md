# Hot and cold holding, cooling and reheating
Scope: Ready to Eat, Kitchens

Document AZ-SOP-R02 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Hot food is held at 60°C or above, and discarded 2 hours after the hold begins. Cold ready-to-eat food (salads, kachumbari, sauces) is held at 5°C or below.
2. Never keep food in the danger zone, 5–60°C, for more than 2 hours in total. Display only what sells within 2 hours.
3. Cooling: cool leftovers from 60°C to 21°C within 2 hours, and from 21°C to 5°C within the next 4 hours. Use shallow trays, ice baths and the chiller. Label with name, date and time.
4. Reheat once only, to 74°C throughout and serve within 2 hours. Never reheat in a hot-holding unit.
5. Leftover cooked meat: use within 24 hours in the chiller. Do not sell unlabelled food.
## Records
Hot-hold log (every 2 hours), cooling log.
