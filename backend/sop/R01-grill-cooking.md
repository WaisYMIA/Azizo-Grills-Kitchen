# Grill and nyama choma cooking
Scope: Ready to Eat

Document AZ-SOP-R01 · Version 1.0 · Owner: Azizo Quality and Operations · Review every 6 months

> Internal Azizo standard, written to align with Kenyan food-safety law (Food, Drugs and Chemical Substances Act, Meat Control Act, Public Health Act and county by-laws) and with HACCP / Codex Alimentarius principles. It is not a government certificate. Have a food-safety professional or the county public health officer review it before you rely on it.

## Steps
1. Cook from fresh or properly thawed meat only. Marinate in the chiller, never at room temperature, and use a clean container for each batch.
2. Core temperatures to reach and hold for at least 15 seconds: poultry and minced meat 74°C; pork 71°C; whole cuts of beef and goat 63°C with a 3-minute rest, or well done on request; fish 63°C; sausages 74°C. Check with a probe thermometer, in the thickest part, and write it down at least once per batch.
3. Use separate tongs, boards and plates for raw and cooked. Wash the probe between uses.
4. Grill surfaces are scraped and cleaned between batches. Oil is changed on schedule and filtered; discard oil that smokes, smells or foams.
5. No fuel (charcoal, gas) is stored near food. Keep an extinguisher and fire blanket near the grill, and train staff to use them.
## Records
Batch cooking log with temperature, time, cook's initials.
## If something goes wrong
Undercooked: return to the grill until it reaches the limit. Cooked food on the floor or in contact with raw: discard.
